<b>Problems:</b> 
As the number of gaming consoles and video titles continues to expand, small video game stores face growing challenges in accurately tracking sales and managing inventory. Many still rely on traditional practices, such as point-of-sale systems combined with manual stock counts, which often result in errors and inefficiencies. Implementing an online database system will make shopping easier for customers and help stores manage their inventory more effectively.
 
<b>Scope:</b>
The database will maintain and enforce data related to the product catalog, inventory, customers, orders, and suppliers. It will also integrate with an e-commerce platform to enable online purchasing, ensure accurate inventory tracking, streamline restocking processes, and provide reliable access to information for both customers and store staff.
